The way I approach engineering is shaped by a set of deliberate principles developed through building real systems — defining clarity before action, using code as a tool for exploration, pushing beyond abstractions to understand what truly happens beneath the surface, and maintaining precise standards that protect reliability. Together, they form a mindset focused not just on shipping features, but on building systems that are intentional, understandable, and dependable.
